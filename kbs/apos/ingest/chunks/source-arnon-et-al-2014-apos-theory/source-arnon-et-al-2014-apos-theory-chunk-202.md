5.4.1.2 Class Discussion
Since the purpose of the ﬁrst iteration of the cycle was to facilitate the development
of a Process conception of decimal strings, the instructor’s goal was to support
reﬂective activity that would help students to interiorize the external Action of
listing the digits of a decimal string. Beyond having the students share their
descriptions of the mystery strings, this called for the class discussion to focus
squarely on consideration of the functioning of View, with particular attention paid
to what View returns for different values of n.
