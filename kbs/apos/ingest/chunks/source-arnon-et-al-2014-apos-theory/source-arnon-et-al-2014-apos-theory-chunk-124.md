When this happens, the genetic decomposition needs to be reﬁned to reﬂect what
has been found empirically.
Revisions of the genetic decomposition may lead to changes in instruction, as
well as provide an opportunity for further empirical analysis. The cycle of instruc-
tion ! analysis ! reﬁnement can be repeated until it is determined that the
reﬁnement adequately describes students’ constructions and leads to effective
instruction.
