In general, the ﬁrst-order propositions in the logic Schema described above are
Processes that originate fr om interiorizing Actions (c onjunctions, disjunctions,
implications, negations) o n declarative statements (Ob jects). For example, the
formation of the disjunction P _ Q can be described as an Action on the statements
P; Q. The Action, which goes well beyond simply putting these symbols into a
disjunction expression, involves the co nstruction of a mental image of the two
statements and the determination of the truth or falsity of the disjunction in
various situations. Through interiorization, the Action is transformed into a Process
